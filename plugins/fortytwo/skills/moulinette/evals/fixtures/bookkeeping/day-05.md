# Day 05 — Trial balance

## Objective
Turn a month of raw transactions into a trial balance that proves itself.

## Task
Below are March's transactions for a one-person consultancy. Produce `trial-balance.md` containing:

1. A ledger showing every transaction posted to its accounts, debit and credit.
2. A trial balance listing each account's closing balance in the correct column.
3. The debit and credit totals, which must be equal.

Transactions:
- 01/03 — Owner invests 10,000 cash into the business.
- 04/03 — Buys a laptop for 1,800 cash.
- 09/03 — Invoices a client 4,500 for consulting, on 30-day terms.
- 15/03 — Pays 600 rent in cash.
- 22/03 — Client pays 4,500 invoice in cash.
- 27/03 — Buys 250 of office supplies on credit.
- 31/03 — Pays 120 for software subscriptions in cash.

Constraint: every figure in the trial balance must be traceable to a posting in your ledger. No plug figures.

## Self-check
1. Total debits equal total credits.
2. Every transaction appears exactly twice, once as a debit and once as a credit.
3. Cash's closing balance matches what you get by adding the cash movements independently.
